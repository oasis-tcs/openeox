# Red Hat's Use Case and Value Scenarios
- Contributor: Red Hat
- Based on Red Hat's internal Product Data Service, the system of record for product/version/lifecycle
  data, generalized here into vendor-neutral, OpenEoX-relevant use cases.
- Several scenarios below use Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform (OCP) 4 as a concrete worked
  example of a multi-phase support model (General Availability, Full Support, Maintenance Support,
  Extended Update Support, End-of-Life).

They are:
- [Discover all currently supported product streams for a product family](Scenario-01.md)
- [Track the full support-phase schedule of a product version](Scenario-02.md)
- [Resolve dependent products lifecycle linking between a product and its base product](Scenario-03.md)
- [Get notified ahead of a lifecycle or support-phase transition](Scenario-04.md)
- [Map a vulnerable component to its product, version, and End-of-Security-Support status](Scenario-05.md)
- [Audit lifecycle/security-support compliance across a product portfolio](Scenario-06.md)
- [Discover planned future releases and their expected GA date](Scenario-07.md)
